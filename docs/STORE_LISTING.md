## Detailed description

```
SkoolCal Sync extracts every event from a Skool community calendar, so you don't have to open each event and download its .ics file by hand.

WHAT IT DOES
• Detects the calendar automatically when you open a Skool community's Calendar tab.
• Collects each event's title, description, exact start and end time, timezone, meeting link (Zoom / Google Meet / Teams), host and event ID.
• Detects recurring series and can collapse them into proper calendar rules.
• Exports everything as one .ics file, or one .ics per event.
• Syncs directly to Google Calendar using your own Google account.

IMPORTS ANYWHERE
The .ics files it produces are standard (RFC 5545) and import into Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, Microsoft Outlook, Outlook.com, Yahoo and Thunderbird.

GOOGLE CALENDAR SYNC
Connect once with your own Google OAuth client ID and SkoolCal Sync will add the events you select to the calendar of your choice. Re-running a sync never creates duplicates — events are matched on their original Skool ID.

PRIVACY FIRST
• Everything runs locally in your browser.
• No analytics, no tracking, no third-party servers.
• Your data leaves your device only when you press "Sync to Google Calendar", and then it goes straight to Google using your credentials.
• No remote code: everything ships inside the extension.

DATE RANGES
Filter with one tap: Today, This week, This month, Next 30 days, This year, Till year end, or All time. Months are only fetched when you export, so simply browsing the calendar sends no extra requests.

REQUESTS ONLY WHAT IT NEEDS
storage, identity, notifications, alarms, plus access to skool.com and Google's APIs. No tabs, no scripting, no "read all sites", no downloads permission.

Not affiliated with or endorsed by Skool.com.
```
